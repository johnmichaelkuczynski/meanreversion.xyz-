Mean Reversion is the first main content on the page, including its score area
and a large chart visible even before a ticker is entered. Its ticker entry
belongs below that chart. The existing SOXL/SRH content belongs below the
entire Mean Reversion workspace.

**Why:** The user repeatedly required Mean Reversion at the absolute top,
superseding the earlier SOXL-first page order.

**How to apply:** Do not let the account header, SOXL quote, diagnostics or
SOXL signal precede Mean Reversion. Within the lower SOXL section, preserve
the daily signal followed by its actual interactive drawing/projection chart.

Chart controls must start expanded and readable, with the header fully below the incline bar. Do not auto-shrink the panel to the small portion of the iframe visible when it first enters the viewport.

**Why:** The user rejected the clipped header and compressed initial panel as unrecognizable. Allow deliberate user resizing/collapse, but not automatic miniature controls.

Keep signal cards compact, with large signal words and readable plain-English
explanations. Keep validation statistics, sample-size warnings, range metadata
and scale legends in collapsed details rather than tiny footnotes beneath BUY.

**Why:** The user repeatedly said that technical text beneath NO EDGE belongs
in the dropdown and explicitly asked to remove the micro-font beneath BUY.

Put maintenance information about background updates and saved data at the very
bottom of the page, collapsed, not above the investor-facing signals or chart.

**Why:** The user explicitly said it belongs "way at the bottom" so it does not
distract people deciding whether to buy SOXL.

Never show colored warning panels or boilerplate financial/legal disclaimers
in the app or newly generated reports. A missing price should have no warning
banner or setup commentary. Preserve plain operational feedback and timestamps.

**Why:** The user repeatedly demanded removal and explicitly said:
"No colored warnings. No disclaimers. Ever."

**How to apply:** Keep notifications neutral, omit quote-unavailability notices
and boilerplate disclaimer blocks, and do not change prices or calculations.

Put the custom-stock ticker input in the console's first content area, formerly
occupied by the CHART CONTROLS heading; in a wide console it sits beside benchmarks.

**Why:** The user explicitly pointed to that blank area as the intended input
location after being unable to find it among the benchmark controls.
